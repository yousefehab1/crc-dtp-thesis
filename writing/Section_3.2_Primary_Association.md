## 3.2 Primary association: the drug-tolerant persister signature and colorectal cancer outcome (univariable)

The hypothesis makes a first prediction: a drug-tolerant persister (DTP) programme that reflects a shared cancer-stress state should track patient outcome in primary colorectal cancer. This section tests that association without adjustment for clinicopathology, across two cohorts and two endpoints, overall survival (OS) and recurrence-free survival (RFS). Each contrast ran first in the whole cohort, then in the adjuvant-treated subset, where cytotoxic pressure should select most strongly for a persister state. The three DTP scores (Up, Down and Composite) and the six reference signatures defined in Methods 2.2 were run through the same tests, so that the specificity of any DTP result could be judged against the related stemness, regeneration and proliferation programmes. A Mann-Whitney test gave the rank-biserial effect size, a median-split Kaplan-Meier gave the display separation, and a Cox model on the continuous score gave the inference, summarised by the concordance index. All p-values carry Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Direction held throughout: a higher Up score marked shorter survival and earlier recurrence.

In GSE39582 (Affymetrix microarray) the Up score separated both endpoints. Rank-biserial effect sizes reached 0.20 for overall survival (FDR = 0.009) and 0.20 for recurrence (FDR = 0.003), and the Cox models ranked patients above chance at both (per-SD HR 1.44, 95% CI 1.19 to 1.73 for OS; concordance 0.586 for OS, 0.580 for RFS, FDR = 0.001 each). Median-split Kaplan-Meier separation was significant for recurrence (log-rank FDR = 0.027) and near the threshold for overall survival (FDR = 0.064). The Composite score tracked the Up score at lower effect (OS 0.18, FDR = 0.014; RFS 0.14, FDR = 0.026). The Down score returned nothing at either endpoint (all FDR > 0.3), so the up-regulated component carried the association.

TCGA-COAD (RNA-seq) held the same direction but a weaker whole-cohort overall survival signal. The Up score separated recurrence, significant by Cox and log-rank though only borderline on the Wilcoxon screen (effect size 0.22, Wilcoxon FDR = 0.064; log-rank FDR = 0.021; concordance 0.584, Cox FDR = 0.023), and only approached significance for overall survival (effect size 0.18, FDR = 0.092; concordance 0.588, FDR = 0.073). Composite and Down reached nothing for overall survival. Recurrence was the endpoint that replicated across platforms, whereas whole-cohort overall survival stayed modest in GSE39582 and borderline in TCGA-COAD.

The adjuvant-treated subset of GSE39582 (n = 240; 50 OS events, 81 RFS events) sharpened the association. The Up score reached its largest overall-survival effect here (effect size 0.32, FDR = 0.005; log-rank FDR = 0.013; per-SD HR 1.59, 95% CI 1.22 to 2.08; concordance 0.648, FDR = 0.005), above the whole-cohort value on effect size, hazard ratio and concordance. Composite matched the pattern (OS effect size 0.28, FDR = 0.010; concordance 0.627, FDR = 0.010). The Up score stayed associated with recurrence (effect size 0.25, FDR = 0.018; log-rank FDR = 0.012), while the Composite recurrence signal fell short (FDR = 0.167). The adjuvant-treated subset of TCGA-COAD held only 23 patients with an adjuvant flag (5 OS, 13 RFS events) and failed the Mann-Whitney testability gate; its Cox models were underpowered and non-significant, and this section does not interpret them.

Hazard ratios are reported per one standard deviation of the score, for the reasons given in Methods 2.6. The per-SD hazard ratio ran from 1.35 to 1.44 in the whole cohorts and reached 1.59 in the adjuvant-treated subset, and concordance between 0.58 and 0.65 marks a modest, repeatable ability of the continuous score to rank patients by outcome. This is statistically robust but should not be read as clinically decisive: a concordance of 0.58 to 0.65 is reliably above the 0.50 of chance yet well short of the 0.70 or higher generally expected of a stand-alone clinical prognostic tool, so the score is a real but modest discriminator rather than a ready-made classifier.

The reference signatures set the DTP result against related programmes. The fetal intestinal programme came closest to the Up score, enriched in poor-outcome patients and significant at both endpoints in GSE39582 (overall survival effect size 0.17, FDR = 0.013, concordance 0.578, Cox FDR = 0.002; recurrence effect size 0.16, FDR = 0.012, Cox FDR = 0.008), though not in TCGA-COAD after correction (overall survival FDR = 0.767; recurrence effect size 0.17, FDR = 0.102, with a nominal log-rank FDR = 0.039). That overlap fits a persister state built on fetal-regenerative reprogramming rather than an isolated gene set. The MYC module pointed the other way, higher in the better-outcome patients, and was significant at both endpoints in GSE39582 (overall survival effect size 0.16, FDR = 0.015, Cox FDR = 0.031; recurrence effect size 0.18, FDR = 0.007, Cox FDR = 0.035). The remaining four signatures, the regenerative stem cell, revSC, IBD and crypt-base columnar cell (CBC) programmes, survived correction in no cohort at either endpoint. The prognostic signal therefore sat on the Up and fetal axes and did not generalise to the stem-cell, revival or inflammatory scores, which weakens any account in which any proliferation- or stemness-related score would split outcome.

The conclusion of this section is the central result of the thesis: the cell-line-derived persister score has genuine, reproducible prognostic value in colorectal cancer patients, with a higher up score marking worse survival and earlier recurrence across two independent cohorts and two platforms. The up-regulated component carried this signal, not the down component. Recurrence showed the association most clearly in the whole cohort, and overall survival showed it best once the analysis narrowed to adjuvant-treated patients, while whole-cohort overall survival was the weakest contrast and borderline in TCGA-COAD, a point the Discussion returns to. Figures 4 and 5 display these results, and the full set of values is reported as **Appendix Table A5**. Because a pooled association can be carried by, or confined to, particular molecular or clinical subsets, the next question is where within the disease the signal actually sits.
