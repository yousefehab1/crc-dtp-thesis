## 3.1 Cohort and data overview

The Results pursue one primary aim and two exploratory extensions, each on its own data, over a shared signature and scoring core (Figure 3). The drug-tolerant persister signature, its up, down and composite scores together with six reference signatures, was scored in every dataset by ssGSEA single-sample scoring, so the same programme is measured the same way throughout. The primary aim is to establish whether the signature carries clinical prognostic value in colorectal cancer and whether that value is independent of established variables (Sections 3.2 to 3.4). Two further strands then explore, without expecting a particular answer, whether that prognostic value extends to other cancer types (Section 3.5) and whether the programme is enriched in metastasis (Section 3.6), before Section 3.7 draws them together.

The colorectal strand used two independent cohorts, GSE39582 (Affymetrix microarray, about 585 patients) and TCGA-COAD (RNA-seq, 455 patients), each with overall survival and recurrence endpoints, analysed in the whole cohort, the adjuvant-treated subset, and within CMS, PDS, stage and microsatellite subgroups. The pan-cancer strand drew on the TCGA Clinical Data Resource: of 33 solid-tumour cohorts, two were removed as non-solid where the persister notion does not apply (TCGA-LAML and TCGA-DLBC), five fell below the 100-patient threshold (ACC, MESO, UVM, UCS and CHOL), and one dropped below it after the clinical join (TCGA-KICH, 66 patients), leaving 25 cohorts and about 8,900 patients. The metastasis strand used GSE50760, paired RNA-seq of matched normal mucosa, primary tumour and liver metastasis from roughly 18 patients. The datasets, their sizes and the analyses each entered are laid out in Figure 3. The primary aim and the two exploratory extensions, and what each returned, are summarised in Table 3.0 and developed in the sections that follow.

Table 3.0. The primary aim, the two exploratory extensions, and what the analysis found.

| Aim | Question | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Primary: clinical value in colorectal cancer | Does the persister score predict outcome, independently of established variables? (Sections 3.2 to 3.4) | Yes: prognostic and independent |
| Exploratory: other cancer types | Does that prognostic value extend to other solid tumours? (Section 3.5) | Partial: strong only in a few tissues |
| Exploratory: metastasis | Is the programme enriched in metastatic tissue? (Section 3.6) | Not supported at the bulk level |

With the cohorts, scores and endpoints defined, the primary aim is addressed first, asking the most direct question: is the persister score associated with outcome in primary colorectal cancer?
