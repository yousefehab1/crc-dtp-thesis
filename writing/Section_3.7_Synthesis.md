## 3.7 Synthesis of results

One signature, scored the same way in every dataset, was tested against the three predictions of the hypothesis (Figure 3). In primary colorectal cancer the DTP up programme tracked outcome in two independently processed cohorts, clearest for recurrence in the whole cohort and strongest for overall survival in the adjuvant-treated subset, and it added prognostic information beyond stage, microsatellite status and molecular subtype. Across 25 cancer types the direction leaned the same way, toward higher hazard, but reached significance in only a minority of cohorts, and the pooled effect was small and of near-chance discrimination. In the metastatic compartment, once liver contamination was controlled, the up programme showed no enrichment by bulk analysis, while the programmes that moved were led by Myc. The three predictions were therefore met to different degrees, strongest for the primary-outcome prediction, partial for cross-tissue generalisation, and absent at the bulk level for metastasis. The Discussion weighs what this means for the hypothesis.
